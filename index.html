Daniel Choate

I am a PhD candidate in the Automated Systems and Robotics (ASAR) Lab at Tufts University, led by Dr. Jason Rife. My research focuses on high-integrity navigation for autonomous vehicles, specializing in computer vision, sensor fusion, and embedded systems.

I have experience designing real-time systems with microcontrollers, wireless communication systems through MQTT/BLE, IMU sensors, and LiDAR. My projects include balancing robots, secure delivery robots, and simultaneous localization and mapping (SLAM) frameworks.

With experience in tools like ROS, Open3D, and OpenCV, I integrate 3D visualization and image processing into robotics workflows. I’m skilled in Python and C++, with expertise in real-time state estimation solutions.
